2018

Year End The big news for 2018 was the new cable connection: 30Mbps rather than about 2.5Mbps.

But it came at an emotional price: I lost the static IP (216.138.233.67) that I have had since I first got broadband in 2000. I also moved all the servers off of YOW. Losing complete control over my email has been hard.

The upside is that Blackberry now allows me to read my email at work via web-client (not imap though). I can also send email to my home account. This was always a sticky point with hosting my own email in the later years.

On a scarier note, we had three power outages this year. To put this in perspective, we had three power outages between September 2001 (when I started keeping track) and 2017. I don't think we had any outages from 1996 to 2003... Let' hope this was "just a bad year".

I decided to expand the year end to my home computers in general rather than just YOW. I bought a new SSD for my main laptop, so now I am Windows-less.

On a very sad note I dropped XEmacs support. I realized I was spending a lot of time supporting a platform I am no longer using. I have been running XEmacs since the mid-90s. But the last couple of years it was easier to switch to GNU Emacs for work... and that spread to home.
