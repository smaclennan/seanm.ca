M998 HMMWV

HMMWV (pronounced hum-vee) stands for High Mobility Multipurpose Wheeled Vehicle. While this truck is the direct replacement for the M151 in the US, it also serves other purposes and is in a different class from the other jeeps.

The civilian HMMWV is called the HUMMER. Hummer is "lobster" in German.
