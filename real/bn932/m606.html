M606

The M606 was the first truck to use the F-head "Hurricane" engine. The F-Head is a much taller engine than the L-Head and the hood had to be raised to accommodate it.

From Nelson Dionne:

The M606 was basically a foreign aid item, and was used all over the world. They were quite common in RVN and Thailand, usually with a "Clasped hands" logo on them. (this logo was reproduced and on sale quite some time back in Supply Line).
