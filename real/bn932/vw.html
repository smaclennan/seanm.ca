VW Type 82 Kuebelwagen

The German Kuebelwagen was produced by VW from 1939 to 1945. It was used during the war to perform much the same duties as the MB. While it was a larger vehicle than the MB, it was much lighter, mainly due to its unibody construction.

The Kubelwagen was also two wheel drive rather than four.

The Kuebelwagen is a two wheel drive vehicle. It has a four speed manual transmission with overdrive.
