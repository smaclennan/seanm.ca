TRUCK, UTILITY, LIGHT, 4X4, ILTIS CANADIAN

The Iltis is the Canadian replacement for the M151A2 built in Canada by Bombadier.

History

1976
The West German Armed Forces sends out proposals for a 4x4 vehicle that can carry a 500kg payload both on and off road. Daimler-Benz and Volkswagen build prototypes.
1977
The Volkswagen Iltis is chosen for production. 8,800 vehicles produced from 1977 to 1981.
1981
Bombadier purchases all the rights to the Iltis, along with the tooling equipment, from Volkswagen.
1983
DND orders 1,900 Iltis from Bombadier.
1984
Belgium orders 2,500 Iltis.
1985
Vehicles delivered to DND.

Engine

The Iltis has a four cylinder OHC engine which develops 75 bhp @ 5,000 rpm.

Drive Train

The Iltis has a manual transmission with 5 speeds. However, only gears 1-4 are used for normal operation. There is a movable sleeve on the the gear shift to access the fifth gear which is, in reality, not a fifth gear but a super low first gear.

The Iltis also has selectable 4wd. There is a small lever on the floor between the driver's and passenger's seat located next to the passenger's seat that shifts between 4wd and 2wd. This switching is purely mechanical. When you put the Iltis into 2wd it goes into front-wheel-drive. Engaging the 4wd lever will engage the back wheels.

Between the driver's and passenger's seat located next to the driver's seat is another small lever which switches the differential lock in and out. This switching is purely mechanical. Diff lock is normally left in the out position.

All gears, including super low first, are available in 2 or 4wd. In addition, diff lock is also available in 2 or 4wd.

Suspension

The Iltis has overhead semi-elliptical leaf springs with double acting shock absorbers.

It looks like it has four-wheel independent suspension, but with springs running the width of the body instead of the normal coils. The Iltis also has the axle tucked up into the body with smaller axles going up to it just like the M998. This gives it much better ground clearance (an extra 2" over the M38A1).

Notes

Some "facts" from looking at an Iltis:

  • all steel construction
  • 24V electrical system
  • slave receptacle near drivers left foot
  • integral roll cage
