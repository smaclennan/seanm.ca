Fender Limited Edition Standard Precision Bass (MIM)

I bought this bass used off a TalkBass member in February 2005. The original owner bought it in October 2004 from Guitar Center. "It is a special run made for Guitar Center. Only Natural finish MIM Precision I've ever seen." The serial number, MZ3xxxxx, means it was built in 2003/2004. Only 250 where made.

This is to be my "rock" bass. I disconnected the tone control, since I have more control at the amp anyway! I almost removed the volume control, but I use it to turn the bass off between sets.

Upgrades/mods

Original owner:

  • Seymour Duncan Basslines SPB-3 Quarter-Pound for P pickups.
  • Black pearl pickguard.

Mine (in roughly chronological order):

  • Flatwound strings.
  • Black pickguard.
  • Quality volume pot.
  • Disconnected tone pot and cap.
  • Filed down fret ends.
  • New GraphTech Trem-nut.
    On the original nut the slots where shifted towards the G string.
  • Shielded.
  • 2019: The clear coat cracked badly; large bits fell off at a gig.
    Removed all the clear coat and used Tru Oil.
