A Short History of the Ripper's

  • July 2007 - Vince puts an ad in TheOM(RIP) looking for a bass player and drummer.
  • Nov 24, 2007 - First charity gig at The Point in Constance Bay. Al Cockerell on drums and Whiskey Mike on lead guitar.

    Found the original post to ?? (probably TheOM):
    # FOODSTOCK ...Benefit for the West Carleton Emergency Foodbank. Sat. Nov. 24th..Great bands and grub.. Fun starts at 2:00pm.. Special guests .. Gord Black duo..Vinnie and the Rippers..The Fabulous Flashbax..and finish off the evening with rockin'blues with WCBlues.. It all takes place at THE POINT LOUNGE in beautiful downtown Constance Bay. Non perihable food items and cash donations would be appreciatred.. Come and join the fun for a great cause. www.wcblues.com
  • June 14, 2008 - Gig in Osgoode. Hank Alton joins the band on Drums. Yes, Hank is also an accomplished drummer. After this gig Hank switched to lead guitar.
  • Sep 16, 2008 - rippers.ca lives!
  • Dec 4, 2008 - First MS Rockathon at Greenfields. Mike Thompson joins the band on drums.
  • May 14, 2011 - Gig at the Mill Tavern with Roger Brown on drums. Mike was having problems with his hands so Roger replaced him.
  • Sep 2016 - Roger leaves the band.
  • Jan 28, 2017 - Gig at the Mill Tavern with Alain Achkar on drums.
  • May 14, 2017 - Vince splits up current version of the band.
