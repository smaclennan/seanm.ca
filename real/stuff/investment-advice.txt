Investment advice (2008)

I thought I would pass on some investing advice in these tough times....

If you had purchased $1,000 of AIG stock one year ago, you would have
$42 left. With Lehman, you would have $6.60 left. With Fannie or
Freddie, you would have less than $5 left.

But if you had purchased $1,000 worth of beer one year ago, drank all
of the beer, then turned in the cans for the aluminum recycling
refund, you would have had $214.

Based on the above, the best current investment advice is to drink
heavily and recycle.
