A Draft :-)

Twenty-eight years ago, Herman James, a Tennessee mountain man, was
drafted by the Army.

On his first day in boot camp, the Army issued him a comb.  That
afternoon, an Army barber sheared his head.

On his second day, the Army issued him a toothbrush.  That afternoon,
an Army dentist pulled several of his teeth.

On his third day, he was issued an athletic supporter.  The Army is
still looking for him.
