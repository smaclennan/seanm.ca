 1. You're not offended by the term... Homo Milk.
 2. You understand the phrase "Could you please pass me a serviette. I just
    dropped my poutine on the chesterfield."
 3. You eat chocolate bars, not candy bars.
 4. You drink pop, not soda.
 5. You know that a Mickey and a 2-4 means party at the camp, eh!
 6. You don't care about the fuss with cuba. It is a cheap place to go on
    holidays, not vacation, with good cigars and no Americans.
 7. You know that a pike is a type of fish and not part of a highway.
 8. You drive on a highway, not a freeway.
 9. You have Canadian Tire money in your kitchen drawers.
10. You cried when Mr. Dress-up died recently.
11. You get excited whenever an American television show mentions Canada.
12. You brag to Americas that Pamela Anderson, Shania Twain, Jim Carey, and
    Celine Dion are Canadians.
13. You know that the CEO of American Airlines is Canadian.
14. You know what a touque is.
15. You design your halloween costume to fit over your snowsuit.
16. You know that the last letter of the English alphabet is always
    pronounced "Zed."
17. Your local newspaper covers the national news.
18. You know that when it is 25 degress outside, it is a warm day.
19. You understand the Labatt Blue commercials.
20. You know how to pronounce and spell "Saskatchewan."
21. You perk up when you hear the theme song from "Hockey Night in Canada."
22. You are in Grade 12, not the 12th Grade.
23. "Eh?" is a very important part of your vocabulary, and is more polite
    than "Huh?"
