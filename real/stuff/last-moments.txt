On a Trans-Atlantic flight, a plane passes through a severe storm.
The turbulence is awful, and things go from bad to worse when one wing
is stuck by lightning. One woman in particular loses it.

Screaming, she stands up in the front of the plane. "I'm too young to
die," she wails. Then she yells, "Well, if I'm going to die, I want my
last minutes on earth to be memorable! Is there anyone on this plane
who can make me feel like a WOMAN?"

For a moment there is silence. Everyone has forgotten their own
peril. They all stare, riveted, at the desperate woman in the front of
the plane.

Then a man from Texas stands up in the rear of the plane. He is
handsome: tall, well built, with dark brown hair and hazel eyes.  He
starts to walk slowly up the aisle, unbuttoning his shirt.

.........She gasps................
