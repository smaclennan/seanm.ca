Welcome to seanm.ca - The homepage of Sean MacLennan
----------------------------------------------------

Back in 1996 I got a dialup account (bn932) on the NCF (National
Capital Freenet). You were allowed to make a very small website (it
was really only supposed to be one homepage), so I did.

Then in 2000 I got broadband... a whopping 1Mb/s. It was a game
changer. I bought the domains seanm.ca and m38a1.ca and created static
websites for them that I hosted locally (I had a static IP).

The website grew... until it didn't. I got tired of maintaining the
sites. So I dropped m38a1.ca and seanm.ca became a shadow of its
previous self.

But seanm.ca still exists, and here it is. Or you could do it the
boring way and look at it in a web browser.
